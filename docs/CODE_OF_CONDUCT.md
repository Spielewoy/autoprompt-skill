# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to keep participation in this project open, welcoming, and free from harassment, regardless of identity, background, appearance, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Own mistakes, apologize, and correct them.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Insults, trolling, discrimination, or personal and political attacks
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to a maintainer through the private channel used to coordinate repository access. For conduct on GitHub, you may also use GitHub's [reporting controls](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Do not put sensitive personal information in an issue or pull request.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect the privacy and safety of the reporter, and respond in proportion to the conduct and its impact. Consequences may include a correction, warning, temporary or permanent ban, or removal of project content. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
